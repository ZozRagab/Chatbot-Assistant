Product: Nova X12 Smartphone

The Nova X12 features a 6.5-inch OLED display, triple rear camera system (main, ultra-wide, and telephoto lenses), and a fast-charging 5000mAh battery that reaches 50% charge in just 25 minutes.

The device runs on a powerful octa-core processor with 8GB of RAM, ensuring smooth performance for gaming, streaming, and multitasking. Storage options include 128GB and 256GB variants, both expandable via microSD card.

Water and dust resistance is rated at IP68, allowing the device to withstand submersion in up to 1.5 meters of water for 30 minutes. The phone also includes an in-display fingerprint sensor and facial recognition for secure unlocking.

Color options: Midnight Black, Ocean Blue, Rose Gold
Price: $699 (128GB) / $799 (256GB)
Category: Electronics
