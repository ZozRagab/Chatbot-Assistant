Frequently Asked Questions - Discounts and Promo Codes

Q: How do I apply a promo code?
A: Enter your promo code in the "Discount Code" field at checkout before completing your purchase.

Q: Can I use multiple promo codes on one order?
A: No, only one promo code can be applied per order.

Q: Why isn't my promo code working?
A: Promo codes may be expired, restricted to certain products, or require a minimum purchase amount. Check the terms of the specific code.

Q: Do you offer student discounts?
A: Yes, students can get a 10% discount by verifying their student status through our partner service.

Q: How do I find out about upcoming sales?
A: Sign up for our newsletter to receive notifications about sales, exclusive discounts, and new product launches.
