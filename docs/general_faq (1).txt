General Product and Account FAQ

Product freshness
All produce, dairy, and bakery items are sourced daily from local suppliers.
Each product listing shows its typical shelf life so you know what to
expect when it arrives.

Product availability
If an item is out of stock, it will be marked unavailable on its product
page and cannot be added to your cart. You can enable a "notify me" alert
on the product page to be emailed when it's back in stock.

Substitutions
We do not automatically substitute out-of-stock items. If something in
your order becomes unavailable after you've placed it, we'll contact you
before delivery to offer a substitute or a refund for that item only.

Creating and managing your account
You can update your name, email, phone number, and saved addresses anytime
under Account Settings. Multiple delivery addresses can be saved and
selected at checkout.

Forgotten password
Use the "Forgot Password" link on the login screen to receive a reset link
by email. For security, reset links expire after 30 minutes.

Product reviews
Only customers who have purchased a product can leave a review for it.
Reviews are public and shown on the product page to help other shoppers -
your name is displayed with your review unless you choose to post
anonymously in your account privacy settings.

Bulk or wholesale orders
For orders larger than typical household quantities (e.g. for events or
small businesses), contact support directly - we offer custom pricing and
delivery scheduling for bulk orders.

Contacting support
Support is available daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM through in-app chat,
email, or phone. For order-specific issues, having your order number ready
speeds up resolution.
