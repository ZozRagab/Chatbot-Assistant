Product: Summit Insulated Winter Jacket

The Summit Insulated Jacket is designed for cold-weather performance, featuring a water-resistant outer shell and 600-fill synthetic insulation that retains warmth even in damp conditions. An adjustable hood with faux-fur trim provides extra protection against wind and snow.

The jacket includes an interior chest pocket for valuables, two zippered hand pockets, and elastic cuffs to seal out cold air. A two-way front zipper allows for better mobility when sitting or walking.

This jacket runs slightly large; customers between sizes are advised to size down for a more fitted look, or keep their usual size for a roomier fit that allows layering underneath.
