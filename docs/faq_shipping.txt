Frequently Asked Questions - Shipping

Q: How long does standard shipping take?
A: Standard shipping takes 5-7 business days within the United States.

Q: Do you offer express shipping?
A: Yes, express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for an additional fee of $15.

Q: Do you ship internationally?
A: Yes, we ship to over 40 countries. International shipping takes 10-15 business days and costs vary by destination.

Q: Is there free shipping?
A: Yes, orders over $50 qualify for free standard shipping within the United States.

Q: How do I track my order?
A: Once your order ships, you will receive a tracking number via email. You can use this to track your package on our website or the carrier's website.
