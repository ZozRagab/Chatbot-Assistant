Payment Methods and Vouchers

Accepted payment methods
- Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Meeza)
- Cash on delivery
- Mobile wallets (Vodafone Cash, InstaPay)
- Store credit from approved returns

Is my payment information secure?
Yes. We never store your full card number on our servers - all card
payments are processed through a PCI-compliant payment gateway, and your
account only stores a masked reference to your card for convenience.

Using a voucher code
Enter your voucher code at checkout in the "Promo Code" field before
placing your order. The discount is applied automatically if the code is
valid and the order meets the voucher's minimum requirements. Only one
voucher can be applied per order.

Why is my voucher not working?
Common reasons a voucher fails to apply:
- The voucher has expired.
- The order total is below the voucher's minimum spend requirement.
- The voucher is restricted to specific categories or first-time customers.
- The voucher has already been used (most codes are single-use per account).

Can I combine multiple vouchers?
No, only one voucher code can be applied per order.

Failed or pending payments
If a card payment shows as "pending" for more than 30 minutes, the charge
was likely not completed - please retry checkout. If your card was charged
but the order does not appear in your account, contact support with your
payment reference number and we'll resolve it within one business day.

Invoices and receipts
A digital receipt is emailed automatically after every completed order.
You can also download past invoices anytime from Order History.
