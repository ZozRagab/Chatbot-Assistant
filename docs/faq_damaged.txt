Frequently Asked Questions - Damaged or Defective Items

Q: What do I do if my item arrives damaged?
A: Contact our support team within 48 hours of delivery with photos of the damage. We will send a replacement or issue a full refund at no cost to you.

Q: Do I need to return a damaged item?
A: In most cases, no. For damaged items we typically issue a replacement or refund without requiring you to ship the item back.

Q: What if the item is defective but not visibly damaged?
A: Defective items are covered under our warranty policy. Contact support to start a warranty claim.

Q: How long does it take to get a replacement?
A: Replacements for damaged items are shipped within 1-2 business days of the claim being approved.

Q: Who pays for return shipping on a damaged item?
A: We cover all return shipping costs when the item arrives damaged or defective.
