Shipping and Delivery

Delivery areas
We currently deliver across Cairo, Alexandria, and surrounding governorates.
Enter your address at checkout to confirm delivery availability for your area.

Delivery timing
- Standard delivery: same-day if ordered before 2:00 PM, next-day otherwise.
- Express delivery: within 2 hours of order confirmation, available in
  central Cairo and Alexandria only, for an additional fee.
- Scheduled delivery: choose a specific 2-hour delivery window up to 5 days
  in advance at checkout.

Delivery fees
- Orders over 300 EGP: free standard delivery.
- Orders under 300 EGP: a flat 25 EGP delivery fee applies.
- Express delivery: additional 40 EGP regardless of order size.

Tracking your order
Once your order status changes to "shipped," you'll receive a tracking
link by SMS and email. You can also check real-time status anytime under
Order History in the app.

Order statuses explained
- Processing: your order is being prepared at the warehouse.
- Shipped: your order has left the warehouse and is on its way.
- Delivered: your order has been handed to you or left at your address.

Missed deliveries
If you're not available at the scheduled time, our driver will attempt
contact and wait up to 10 minutes. If delivery can't be completed, the
order is rescheduled for the next available window at no extra charge.

Payment on delivery
Cash on delivery is available for all standard and scheduled deliveries.
Express deliveries require online payment at checkout.
