Delivery and Order Status

How delivery works
When you place an order you enter the delivery address you want it sent to.
Your order then moves through a series of statuses that you can follow in
your order history until it reaches you. You pay the driver in cash when
the order arrives.

How long does delivery take?
There is no published delivery timeframe. We do not promise same-day,
next-day or any specific number of days, and no estimated delivery time is
given when you place an order. What we can tell you is the delivery time
recorded against your own order once one has been set for it - open the
order in your order history to see it.

Which areas do you deliver to?
There is no published list of delivery areas or cities. Enter the address
you want your order sent to when you place it. The assistant cannot check
or confirm in advance whether a particular area is covered.

Order statuses explained
- Pending: your order has been placed and is waiting to be prepared.
- Placed: your order has been accepted and is being prepared.
- Shipped: your order has left us and is on its way to your address.
- Delivered: your order has reached you.
- Cancelled: the order was cancelled and will not be delivered.

Following your order
Your order history shows the current status of every order you have placed,
along with its order number, total, delivery address and items. Check there
at any time to see where an order has got to.

Delivery fees
No separate delivery fee is added to your order. The total shown on your
order is what you pay the driver.

Express or scheduled delivery
There is no express delivery option and no way to book a specific delivery
window or time slot. Orders are delivered in the normal way after you place
them.

Delivery drivers and contact before delivery
There is no published policy on what happens if you are not at home, how
long a driver waits, or whether you are contacted before delivery. No
information about this is available.

Delivery address changes
The delivery address is fixed to the one you entered when you placed the
order. It cannot be changed afterwards, and the assistant cannot change it
for you - so please check the address carefully before placing an order.

If your order has not arrived
The assistant can look up your order and tell you its current status and
the delivery time recorded against it. Beyond that there is no way to chase
or reschedule a delivery at the moment.
