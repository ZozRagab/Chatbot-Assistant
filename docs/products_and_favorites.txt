Browsing Products, Reviews and Favorites

Product categories
The store is organised into three categories: Fruits, vegetables, and
Packages. Fruits and vegetables are fresh produce. Packages covers
everything that comes packaged - dairy, bakery, beverages, pantry goods,
snacks and frozen items. You can browse any category to see the products
inside it.

Product tags
Products carry tags that describe what they are, such as Milk, Soy-Milk,
Oat-Milk, Sour-Milk, Sour-Cream, Apple and Pear. Tags make it easier to
find related products across categories. Products currently being promoted
carry the sales_deals tag.

What each product listing shows
Every product shows its name, description, price, an image, and how many
units are currently in stock. Listings also show the product's average
customer rating and how many reviews it has received.

Stock and availability
Stock counts are live. If a product's stock has run out you will not be
able to add that quantity to your cart. Stock is shown on the product page
so you can see how many units are available before you order.

Product reviews and ratings
Signed-in customers can leave a review on a product with a star rating and
a written comment. Reviews are public and appear on the product page to
help other shoppers decide.

Who can leave a review
Any signed-in customer can leave a review on a product. You do not have to
have bought the product first.

Your name on your review
Your name is shown next to any review you leave, so other shoppers can see
who wrote it. Reviews cannot be posted anonymously - if you would rather
not have your name displayed on a product page, do not leave a review.

Managing your own reviews
You can edit or delete a review you have written at any time from the
product page. You can only change your own reviews - never anyone else's.

Average rating
A product's average rating is calculated from all the customer reviews it
has received, shown alongside the total number of reviews. A product with
no reviews yet has no rating.

Saving favorite products
You can save any product to your favorites to find it again quickly. Open
the product and add it to your favorites, and it appears in your favorites
list. Removing it from favorites takes it off that list. Product pages show
whether an item is already in your favorites.

Saving favorite categories
You can also favorite a whole category, not just individual products. This
keeps the parts of the store you shop most often within easy reach. Your
favorite categories can be added and removed at any time.
