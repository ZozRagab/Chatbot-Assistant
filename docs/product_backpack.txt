Product: TrailPro 28L Backpack

The TrailPro 28L is a versatile backpack suitable for daily commuting, travel, or light hiking. It features a padded laptop compartment fitting laptops up to 16 inches, along with multiple organizational pockets for cables, pens, and small accessories.

The exterior is made from water-resistant ripstop nylon, protecting contents from light rain and everyday wear. Padded, adjustable shoulder straps and a breathable back panel improve comfort during extended wear, and a sternum strap helps distribute weight evenly.

A dedicated side pocket fits a water bottle, and a hidden back-panel pocket provides secure storage for a passport or wallet while traveling.

Capacity: 28 liters
Color options: Black, Olive Green, Charcoal
Price: $79
