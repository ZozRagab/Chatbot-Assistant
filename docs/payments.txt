Payment

How you pay
Cash on delivery is the only payment method. You pay the driver in cash
when your order arrives at your door. There is no card, wallet or online
payment option.

No payment at checkout
Because every order is cash on delivery, there is no payment step when you
place your order. You choose your items, enter your delivery address, and
place the order - nothing is charged at that point. Payment happens only
when the order is delivered to you.

What you will pay
You pay the order total shown on your order. Have the amount ready for the
driver when your order arrives.

Card payments, wallets and online payment
We do not accept credit or debit cards, mobile wallets, bank transfers or
any other online payment method. Cash on delivery is the only option
available.

Is my payment information stored?
No card or payment details are ever collected or stored, because no online
payment is taken. The only information tied to your order is your contact
details and the delivery address you entered.

Voucher codes, promo codes and discounts
There are no voucher codes, promo codes, coupons or discount codes. There
is no field to enter one at checkout, and none can be applied to an order.

Store credit
Store credit is not offered and cannot be used to pay for an order.

Invoices and receipts
Your order details - order number, items, quantities, prices and total -
are available in your order history in the app at any time.
