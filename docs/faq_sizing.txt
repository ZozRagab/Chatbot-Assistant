Frequently Asked Questions - Sizing

Q: How do I find my size?
A: Each product page includes a size chart with measurements. We recommend measuring yourself and comparing to the chart rather than relying on your usual size, since sizing varies by brand.

Q: What if the item doesn't fit?
A: If the item doesn't fit, you can exchange it for a different size within 30 days at no extra cost, or return it for a full refund.

Q: Do you offer half sizes for shoes?
A: Yes, most footwear is available in half sizes between US 6 and US 12.

Q: Are your clothes true to size?
A: Most items run true to size, but some styles (especially outerwear) run slightly large. Check the product description for specific fit notes.

Q: Can I get sizing advice before ordering?
A: Yes, you can chat with our support team who can help you pick the right size based on your measurements.
