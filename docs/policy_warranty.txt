Warranty Policy

Standard warranty coverage: Electronics purchased from us are covered by a 1-year limited warranty against manufacturing defects, starting from the date of delivery. Clothing, footwear, and accessories are covered by a 90-day limited warranty against manufacturing defects.

What is covered: The warranty covers defects in materials or workmanship that exist at the time of purchase and appear under normal use. This includes issues such as malfunctioning electronic components, faulty stitching, or broken hardware that was not caused by misuse.

What is not covered: The warranty does not cover damage caused by accidents, misuse, unauthorized modifications, normal wear and tear, or exposure to conditions outside the product's intended use (such as water damage to non-waterproof electronics).

Filing a claim: To file a warranty claim, contact our support team with your order number, a description of the defect, and photos or video evidence if applicable. Claims are typically reviewed within 3-5 business days.

Resolution: Approved warranty claims will be resolved through repair, replacement, or store credit, at our discretion, depending on product availability.

Extended warranty: For electronics, an extended warranty plan is available for purchase at checkout, extending coverage up to 3 years from the original purchase date. Extended warranty plans cover the same terms as the standard warranty but for a longer duration.

Transferability: Warranty coverage applies to the product itself and is transferable if the item is given as a gift, provided the original proof of purchase is available.
