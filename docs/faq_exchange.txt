Frequently Asked Questions - Exchanges

Q: How do I exchange an item?
A: Start an exchange through our returns portal by selecting "Exchange" instead of "Return," then choose the new size or color you want.

Q: Is there a fee for exchanges?
A: No, exchanges are free as long as the new item is of equal or lesser value. You will be charged the difference for higher-value items.

Q: How long does an exchange take?
A: Once we receive your original item, the replacement ships within 1-2 business days.

Q: Can I exchange a sale item?
A: Sale items can be exchanged for a different size of the same product, but not for a different product.

Q: What if the size I want is out of stock?
A: If your desired size is unavailable, you will be offered a refund instead of an exchange.
