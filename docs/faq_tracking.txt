Frequently Asked Questions - Order Tracking

Q: How do I track my order?
A: You will receive a tracking number by email once your order ships. Use it on our website's tracking page or the carrier's site.

Q: My tracking number isn't working yet, why?
A: Tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after a label is created.

Q: What does "in transit" mean?
A: "In transit" means the package has left our warehouse and is on its way through the shipping carrier's network toward your address.

Q: My package shows delivered but I don't have it, what do I do?
A: Check with neighbors and around your property first. If it's still missing after 24 hours, contact support to file a lost package claim.

Q: Can I change my delivery address after the order has shipped?
A: Once shipped, we cannot change the delivery address. Contact the shipping carrier directly, as they may be able to reroute it.
