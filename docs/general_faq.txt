General Account FAQ

Creating an account
Register with your first name, last name, email, phone number and a
password. Email addresses must be unique - the app checks whether an email
is already registered before you submit.

Activating your account
After registering you receive an activation email. Your account must be
activated before you can sign in. If the email does not arrive, request a
new one from the activation screen and it will be sent again.

Signing in and staying signed in
Sign in with your email and password. Your session refreshes automatically
while you keep using the app, so you are not asked to sign in repeatedly.
Signing out ends the session on that device.

Forgotten password
Choose "Forgot Password" on the sign-in screen and we email you a reset
code. Enter that code in the app to verify it, then choose a new password.
Note this is a code you type in, not a link you click.

Changing your password while signed in
You can change your password at any time from your account settings by
entering your current password and a new one. This does not require the
email reset code.

Updating your profile
You can update your first name, last name, email, phone number and date of
birth from your account settings at any time.

Preferred categories
Your profile stores a set of preferred categories. Setting these tells us
which parts of the store you are most interested in so the app can put
those products in front of you first. You can change them whenever you like
from your account settings.

Your activity history
Your account keeps a record of your activity in the app so your
information, orders and saved items stay consistent across devices.

Getting help
This assistant is the way to get help. It can answer questions about
products, prices, stock and store policies, and it can look up your own
orders, your cart and your order status.

What the assistant cannot do
It cannot change, cancel or refund an order, and it cannot change a
delivery address once an order is placed. Those actions are not available
at the moment. If you have a problem with an order that needs one of them,
the assistant will not be able to resolve it for you yet.
