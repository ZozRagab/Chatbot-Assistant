Product: BrewCraft Programmable Coffee Maker

The BrewCraft coffee maker allows you to program brew times up to 24 hours in advance, so fresh coffee is ready when you wake up. It features a 12-cup glass carafe with a keep-warm plate that maintains temperature for up to 2 hours after brewing.

An adjustable brew strength setting lets you choose between regular and bold flavor profiles, and a reusable gold-tone filter eliminates the need for paper filters. The water reservoir includes clear measurement markings for accurate brewing every time.

An auto shut-off feature turns the machine off automatically after 2 hours for safety and energy savings. The removable filter basket and carafe are dishwasher safe for easy cleaning.

Capacity: 12 cups
Color options: Black, Stainless Steel
