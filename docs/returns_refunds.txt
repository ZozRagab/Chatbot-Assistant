Return and Refund Policy

We want you to be fully satisfied with every order. If something isn't right,
here's how returns and refunds work.

Eligibility
- Perishable items (produce, dairy, bakery, meat) can be returned within 24
  hours of delivery if they arrive damaged, spoiled, or incorrect.
- Non-perishable items (beverages, pantry goods, packaged snacks) can be
  returned within 7 days of delivery if unopened and in original packaging.
- Items marked "Final Sale" or purchased with a discount voucher applied to
  a clearance item are not eligible for return.

How to request a return
1. Go to your Order History and select the order containing the item.
2. Choose "Request Return" and select the reason (damaged, wrong item,
   spoiled, no longer needed).
3. For damaged or spoiled items, attach a photo if possible - this speeds
   up approval.
4. Submit the request. Most requests are reviewed within 24 hours.

Refund timing
- Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method.
- Card payments: refunds typically appear within 3-5 business days.
- Cash on delivery orders: refunds are issued as store credit, available
  immediately in your account.
- Voucher amounts used on a returned order are restored to your account
  as a new voucher code, valid for 90 days.

Damaged or missing items on delivery
If an item arrives damaged, spoiled, or is missing from your order entirely,
contact support within 24 hours of delivery for a full refund or free
replacement - no return shipment needed for perishable goods.

Order cancellations
Orders can be cancelled free of charge before they enter the "processing"
status. Once an order is "processing" or later, it can no longer be
cancelled through the app - contact support directly and we'll do our best
to accommodate.
