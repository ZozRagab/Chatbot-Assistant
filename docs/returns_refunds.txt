Returns and Refunds

Are returns available?
No. Returns are not available. There is no way to request a return for an
order or for an individual item.

Are refunds available?
No. Refunds are not available. Because every order is paid cash on
delivery, no money is taken before your order reaches you.

Something is wrong with my order
If an item arrives damaged or spoiled, an item is missing, or you received
something you did not order, we are sorry - that is not the experience we
want you to have. The assistant can look up the order for you and confirm
exactly what was on it, the amount and the delivery details, so you have a
clear record of what happened.

What can be done about it right now
Nothing can be actioned on the order itself yet: a replacement, a refund
and a return are all unavailable, so the assistant cannot arrange any of
them for you. It will not promise a resolution it cannot deliver. Since
orders are paid cash on delivery, the best moment to raise a problem is
while the driver is still with you, before you pay.

Refusing an order at the door
Because you pay cash on delivery, nothing is charged until the order is
handed to you. If something is clearly wrong when the driver arrives, you
can raise it with the driver at that point.

Cancelling an order
Orders cannot be cancelled once placed. There is no cancellation option,
and the assistant cannot cancel an order for you. Some orders show a
Cancelled status - that status is applied by the store, not something you
can set yourself. Please check your cart before placing an order.

Store credit and vouchers
Store credit is not offered, and there are no voucher, promo or refund
codes. See the payment FAQ.
