Frequently Asked Questions - Warranty

Q: What is covered under warranty?
A: Electronics are covered for 1 year against manufacturing defects. Clothing and accessories are covered for 90 days against manufacturing defects.

Q: How do I file a warranty claim?
A: Contact support with your order number and a description of the issue. You may be asked to provide photos or a video of the defect.

Q: Does warranty cover accidental damage?
A: No, warranty only covers manufacturing defects, not accidental damage, misuse, or normal wear and tear.

Q: Can I purchase extended warranty?
A: Yes, extended warranty plans are available for electronics at checkout, extending coverage up to 3 years.

Q: Is the warranty transferable if I give the item as a gift?
A: Yes, warranty coverage is tied to the product, not the original purchaser.
