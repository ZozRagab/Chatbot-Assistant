Product: SoundWave Pro Wireless Headphones

The SoundWave Pro delivers immersive audio with active noise cancellation, reducing ambient noise by up to 95%. The over-ear design uses memory foam ear cushions for extended comfort during long listening sessions.

Battery life provides up to 30 hours of playback with noise cancellation enabled, or 40 hours with it disabled. A quick 10-minute charge provides up to 5 hours of additional playback.

The headphones connect via Bluetooth 5.2 for a stable, low-latency connection, and also include a 3.5mm auxiliary cable for wired use. Built-in microphones allow for clear calls, and touch controls on the ear cup let you adjust volume, skip tracks, and answer calls without reaching for your phone.

Color options: Matte Black, White, Navy Blue
