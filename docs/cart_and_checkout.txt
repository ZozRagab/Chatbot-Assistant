Your Cart and Placing an Order

Adding items to your cart
Add a product to your cart by choosing it and the quantity you want. Your
cart shows every item with its name, image, unit price, the quantity you
chose, and a running total for the whole cart.

Changing quantities or removing items
You can update the quantity of anything already in your cart at any time
before you place your order. Reducing a quantity or setting it to none
takes that item out of your cart.

Price changes while items sit in your cart
Prices can change while a product is still in your cart. When that happens
the item is flagged in your cart so you can see the price is no longer the
one you first added. Always check your cart total before placing the order:
the price you pay is the current price shown at checkout, not the price
from when you first added the item.

Placing an order
When you are ready, place the order from your cart and enter the delivery
address you want it sent to. You type the delivery address in at the time
you place the order. Once the order is placed it is given an order number
you can use to refer to it.

Delivery address
The address is entered per order, so you can send different orders to
different places. There is no separate saved address book - enter the
address you want each time you place an order.

Payment at checkout
There is no online payment step when you place an order. Every order is
paid cash on delivery. See the payment FAQ for details.

Is there a minimum order value?
No minimum order value is set. You can place an order for any amount, and
nothing blocks a small order from going through.

Checking your orders
Your order history lists every order you have placed, with its order
number, total, delivery address, the date you placed it, its current
status, and the items it contains. You can open any order to see its full
details.

Changing or cancelling an order
Orders cannot be changed or cancelled once they are placed. There is no way
to stop, amend or cancel an order yourself, and the assistant cannot do it
for you either - that option is not available at the moment. Please check
your cart carefully before you place an order.
