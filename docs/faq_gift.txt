Frequently Asked Questions - Gifts

Q: Do you offer gift wrapping?
A: Yes, gift wrapping is available for $5 per item and can be selected at checkout.

Q: Can I include a gift message?
A: Yes, you can add a personalized gift message for free during checkout.

Q: Do you sell gift cards?
A: Yes, digital gift cards are available in amounts from $25 to $500 and are delivered instantly by email.

Q: Do gift cards expire?
A: No, our gift cards never expire.

Q: Can the recipient see the price I paid?
A: No, gift receipts do not show prices, only the items and order details.
