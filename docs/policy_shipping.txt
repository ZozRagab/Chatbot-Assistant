Shipping Policy

Processing time: Orders are processed within 1-2 business days of being placed, excluding weekends and holidays. You will receive a confirmation email once your order has shipped, including tracking information.

Domestic shipping: We offer standard shipping (5-7 business days) and express shipping (2-3 business days) within the United States. Standard shipping is free for orders over $50; otherwise a flat fee of $5.99 applies. Express shipping costs an additional $15 regardless of order size.

International shipping: We ship to over 40 countries worldwide. International delivery times range from 10-15 business days depending on destination and customs processing. International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on destination and package weight. Customers are responsible for any customs duties or import taxes charged by their country.

Order tracking: A tracking number is provided via email as soon as your order ships. Tracking information may take up to 24 hours to become active in the carrier's system.

Shipping delays: While we strive to meet estimated delivery times, delays can occur due to weather, customs processing, or high-volume periods such as holidays. We are not responsible for delays caused by the shipping carrier once the package has left our warehouse.

Lost or stolen packages: If a package shows as delivered but has not been received, please contact us within 7 days of the delivery date so we can investigate and, if necessary, file a claim with the carrier.

Address accuracy: Please ensure your shipping address is correct before placing an order. We are not responsible for packages delivered to an incorrect address provided by the customer.
