Product: AeroBook Pro 14

The AeroBook Pro 14 is a lightweight laptop designed for professionals and students who need performance without the bulk. It features a 14-inch high-resolution display, a 10-core processor, 16GB of RAM, and a 512GB solid-state drive, delivering fast performance for everyday multitasking, video calls, and light creative work.

Battery life reaches up to 12 hours on a single charge, making it suitable for a full workday without needing to plug in. The aluminum unibody design weighs just 1.3 kg, making it easy to carry in a backpack alongside other items.

Connectivity includes two USB-C ports, one USB-A port, an HDMI output, and a headphone jack. The laptop also supports Wi-Fi 6 for faster and more stable wireless connections.

Color options: Space Gray, Silver
Price: $1,299
