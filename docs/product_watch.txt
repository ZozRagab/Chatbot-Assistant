Product: PulseTrack Smartwatch

The PulseTrack Smartwatch monitors heart rate, blood oxygen levels, sleep quality, and daily activity, syncing all data to a companion smartphone app. The 1.4-inch AMOLED touchscreen display is bright and easy to read even in direct sunlight.

Battery life lasts up to 7 days on a single charge with typical use, or up to 2 days with the always-on display enabled. The watch is water-resistant up to 50 meters, making it suitable for swimming and water-based workouts.

Over 100 workout modes are built in, including running, cycling, swimming, and strength training, each with automatic detection for common exercises. Smart notifications for calls, texts, and app alerts appear directly on the watch face.

Color options: Black, Silver, Rose Gold
Band material: Silicone (additional leather and metal bands sold separately)
Price: $179
