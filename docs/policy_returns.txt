Return Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you are not, we accept returns under the following conditions.

Eligibility: Items must be returned within 30 days of the delivery date. Products must be unused, unworn, and in their original packaging with all tags attached. Proof of purchase, such as an order confirmation or receipt, is required for all returns.

Non-returnable items: Perishable goods, digital downloads, gift cards, and personal care items that have been opened cannot be returned for hygiene and safety reasons.

Refund process: Once we receive and inspect your returned item, we will notify you of the approval or rejection of your refund. Approved refunds are processed within 3-5 business days and issued to the original payment method. Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to our error or a defective product.

Return shipping costs: Customers are responsible for return shipping costs unless the item was received damaged, defective, or incorrect. In those cases, we provide a prepaid return label at no cost.

Late or missing refunds: If you haven't received a refund after 5 business days, first check your bank account again, then contact your credit card company as it may take some time before the refund is officially posted. If you've done this and still have not received your refund, contact our support team.
